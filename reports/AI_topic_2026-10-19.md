## Key Announcements by Organization

### Meta
*   **Muse Spark Open:** Meta released Muse Spark Open, an 816-billion-parameter open-weight AI model. It is described as the "most capable U.S. open-weight model to date" and is designed for coding and agentic work. Notably, this model can run on two Apple Mac Studio computers, requiring significant computing power but not necessarily a data center. Muse Spark Open is optimized to run on Apple's M5 Ultra chips and is also integrated into Meta's AI glasses for hands-free access.
*   **Muse Wonder:** Meta's first frontier model aimed at agentic and professional work, such as financial document analysis, contracts, and coding. It is available via API and Muse Code. Meta's own tests suggest it is on par with Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra.

### OpenAI
*   **Aurora Models (Blue, Green Alpha, Red Alpha):** OpenAI has released three new models focused on image generation, representing a shift in their focus. This pivot has led to discussions about OpenAI's strategic direction, particularly in light of competitors' advancements in language models.
*   **GPT-6 Astra:** Described as the world's most intelligent and aligned model, GPT-6 Astra excels in computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work. It has achieved state-of-the-art results on various benchmarks, including FrontierMath, ARC-AGI-3, and ExploitBench. GPT-6 Astra is rolling out to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, as well as through the OpenAI API, Microsoft Azure, and AWS Bedrock.
*   **GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Sol and Luna:** These models expand the GPT-6 family, offering cost-efficient performance for everyday work. GPT-6.1 Sol provides near-Astra intelligence at a significantly lower cost. GPT-6 Sol and Luna are available in ChatGPT Work and Codex for paid users, and GPT-6 Luna is accessible to free users in the desktop app.

### Google DeepMind
*   **Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber:** Google DeepMind has launched a new suite of AI models aimed at enhancing efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and cybersecurity capabilities. Gemini 3.6 Flash offers improved performance in coding, knowledge work, and multimodal tasks, while 3.5 Flash-Lite is the most cost-effective option. Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is specifically for cybersecurity and will be available to governments and select partners.
*   **Gemini 4 Argon:** This new frontier model is designed for complex, real-world tasks across domains like coding, finance, legal, cybersecurity, and science. It combines advanced reasoning, visual perception, and expanded domain knowledge. Gemini 4 Argon is available through Google AI Studio and has a 1M token context window.

### Anthropic
*   **Claude Haiku 5.5:** Anthropic has released Claude Haiku 5.5, described as their cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model to date. It is designed for high-volume, cost-sensitive tasks and is significantly more cost-effective than its predecessor.
*   **Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Sonnet 5.5:** Opus 5.5 is Anthropic's new leading model, offering significant performance improvements and cost reductions compared to Opus 5. It shows strong performance in agentic coding and knowledge work. Sonnet 5.5 also sees price reductions for cache reads, making it cheaper for agentic work.
*   **Claude Max and Team Subscribers:** Anthropic is introducing new monthly API credits for these subscribers to support the development of new agents and applications on the Claude Platform.
