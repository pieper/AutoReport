## Executive Summary

This report details freely available liver and kidney datasets suitable for research, with a focus on cancer-related studies. The Kidney and Kidney Tumor Segmentation (KiTS) challenge datasets, particularly KiTS21 and KiTS23, offer extensive CT scans with detailed annotations for kidneys and tumors, including clinical data and non-restrictive licenses. The Liver Tumor Segmentation Benchmark (LiTS) dataset provides a substantial collection of CT scans for liver cancer patients with liver and tumor segmentations. Recent developments include the expansion of the KiTS23 dataset with more cases and varied contrast phases, and AI-generated annotations for broader NCI Imaging Data Commons collections. These datasets are invaluable for developing and validating AI models for diagnosis, segmentation, and treatment outcome prediction.

### Kidney and Kidney Tumor Segmentation (KiTS) Challenge

*   **KiTS23 Expansion:** The most recent iteration, KiTS23, significantly expands the dataset to 599 cases (489 for training, 110 for testing). It introduces cases with "nephrogenic contrast phase" in addition to the "late arterial" phase used previously, offering a richer dataset for segmentation tasks. The training set incorporates data from previous KiTS challenges (KiTS19 and KiTS21).
*   **Commercial Use Availability (KiTS21):** Training data for machine learning algorithms focused on kidney and kidney tumor segmentation is now available for commercial use, with a license purchase. This dataset includes CT scan images of 90 patients.
*   **KiTS19 Data Availability:** The training set for the 2019 Kidney and Kidney Tumor Segmentation Challenge (KiTS19) is available, featuring CT scans for 210 patients with manual semantic segmentations of kidneys and tumors. This data is licensed under CC BY 3.0.

### The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA)

*   **Expanded AI Annotations:** The National Cancer Institute's (NCI) Imaging Data Commons (IDC) has been enhanced with AI-generated annotations for several cancer radiology image collections, including kidney and liver. These annotations, generated by nnU-Net models, have been reviewed by radiologists and are encoded in DICOM format, ensuring interoperability.
*   **TCIA Kidney Datasets:** TCIA hosts collections such as the 2019 Kidney and Kidney Tumor Segmentation Challenge (KiTS19) data, and the TCGA-KIRC (Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma) collection, which links clinical and genomic data with radiological images.
*   **TCIA Liver Datasets:** TCIA also hosts relevant liver datasets, including the HCC-TACE-Seg dataset for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), and data from the Liver Tumor Segmentation (LiTS) challenge, which has been used in other collections.

*   **KiTS-19 Kidney Tumor Segmentation Dataset:** Kaggle also hosts the KiTS-19 dataset, which includes annotations for kidney parenchyma and renal tumors. The license is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with commercial use requiring written permission.

## Notable Papers

*   The **Liver Tumor Segmentation Benchmark (LiTS)** dataset comprises 130 CT scans of liver cancer patients with annotations for liver and tumors, facilitating slice-based segmentation.

*   The **2023 Kidney and Kidney Tumor Segmentation Challenge (KiTS23)** features an expanded training set of 489 cases and a new test set of 110 cases, including data in the nephrogenic contrast phase.
*   The **CT-ORG dataset** contains 140 CT scans with manual segmentations for multiple organs, including the liver and kidneys, and is valuable for training organ segmentation algorithms.
*   A study on **Renal Cell Carcinoma Texture Analysis with the KiTS23 Dataset** evaluated the effectiveness of CT texture analysis in differentiating ccRCC from non-ccRCC renal tumors using the KiTS23 training set.
*   The **Duke Liver Dataset (DLDS)** includes 2146 abdominal MRI series from 105 patients, with segmentations for 310 series, suitable for training segmentation and series classification models.
*   The **LiverHccSeg dataset** provides multiphasic MRI data from 17 patients for whole liver segmentation and 14 patients for HCC tumor segmentation, with annotations from two radiologists.
*   The **LiMT dataset** is a multi-task liver dataset constructed for liver and tumor segmentation, multi-label lesion classification, and lesion detection based on enhanced CT scans.
