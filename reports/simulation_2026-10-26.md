**Executive Summary**

Recent advancements in physics simulation technologies are rapidly expanding their capabilities and accessibility. Open-source platforms are playing a crucial role, offering modern architectures, high accuracy, and programmable customization. Key areas of development include sophisticated soft-tissue simulation for surgical training and bioelectromagnetic simulations for non-invasive brain stimulation. The trend towards more integrated, AI-enhanced, and ubiquitous simulation tools is evident, promising to accelerate innovation across various scientific and industrial sectors.

## Key Announcements by Organization

### Simulation Open Framework Architecture (SOFA)

SOFA is an open-source framework for multi-physics simulation with a strong emphasis on medical applications. It supports interactive and real-time simulations, including soft and rigid body dynamics, fluid dynamics, and heat transfer. SOFA is used by medical research centers and academic hospitals for training, surgical planning, and developing new medical instruments.

### Interactive Medical Simulation Toolkit (iMSTK)

iMSTK is a C++ open-source platform specifically designed for medical simulations, particularly surgical training. It offers real-time physics simulation capabilities, including position-based dynamics, continuous collision detection, and integrated haptics. iMSTK is compatible with Unity and Unreal engines and features a flexible open-source license (Apache 2.0).

### SimNIBS

SimNIBS is an open-source software package for simulating non-invasive brain stimulation techniques like transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) and transcranial electrical stimulation (TES). It allows for realistic calculations of electric fields induced by these methods and supports generating head models from MRI, simulating electric fields, and optimizing stimulation parameters.

### Project Chrono

Project Chrono is a C++ open-source physics-based simulation infrastructure with a platform-independent design. It supports a wide range of applications including robotics, vehicle dynamics, nonlinear finite element analysis, and granular flows. A Python version, PyChrono, is also available. Chrono is released under a BSD-3 license.

### 4C (Comprehensive Computational Community Code)

4C is a comprehensive multi-physics simulation framework that has been released as open-source. It is designed for complex problems in science, engineering, and biomedicine, offering capabilities for single-field and multi-physics coupling. 4C has been applied to model human cardiovascular and respiratory systems and is available under an open-source license with a focus on community contribution.

### Accelerated Understanding

This startup has developed an AI model designed to predict physical phenomena using neural operators, a departure from transformer architectures. Their model can process a massive number of data points, aiming to accelerate scientific discovery in fields like chip design, robotics, extreme weather, and energy.

### PhysicsX

PhysicsX is a platform that unifies simulation, physics AI, data, and engineering applications. It integrates natively with existing engineering tools and is purpose-built for the full product lifecycle, from concept to operations. PhysicsX emphasizes that AI is integral to its platform's operation and is deployed in sectors like semiconductors, aerospace, automotive, and energy.

### MIT's Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL) & Tsinghua University

These institutions have developed "GeoPT," a pre-training approach for AI that teaches models physics more broadly and efficiently. GeoPT enables AI models to simulate how objects respond to forces like wind and water with increased speed and accuracy, with potential applications in vehicle design, robotics, and weather modeling.

## Notable Papers and Software Releases

*   **PhysiCell:** An open-source, agent-based modeling framework for 3D multicellular simulations, incorporating sub-models for cell behavior, microenvironmental conditions, and biotransport.
*   **iMSTK (Interactive Medical Simulation Toolkit):** An open-source platform for physics simulation in the medical field, enabling the development of surgical training content with position-based dynamics and haptics.
*   **CRESSim:** A realistic surgical simulator based on PhysX 5 for the da Vinci Research Kit (dVRK), capable of simulating contact-rich surgical tasks involving soft tissue and body fluids.
*   **Open-tES:** An open-source stimulator for transcranial electrical stimulation, designed for rodent research and shared under a Creative Commons License (CC BY, SA 4.0).
*   **NeuroSimo:** Open-source software for closed-loop EEG- or EMG-guided transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS), allowing researchers to prototype novel stimulation paradigms in Python.
*   **Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS):** Regularly features new open-source software in various fields, including physics simulation packages like ForeFire (wildland-fire spread), PhotonWeave, DeepBench (benchmarking), COBRAPRO (battery modeling), GEOS (subsurface applications), Pooltool (billiards), RidePy (ridepooling), and HW2D (plasma turbulence).
